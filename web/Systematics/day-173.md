## Verse

"But the Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name,
he will teach you all things and bring to your remembrance all that I
have said to you." — John 14:26

## Explanation

We close this short look at the Spirit's person by considering his
distinct role within the Trinity's shared work. Scripture consistently
describes a pattern in how the three persons act: the Father plans and
sends, the Son accomplishes redemption, and the Spirit applies and
completes that redemption in the lives of God's people — teaching,
reminding, illuminating, and, as we'll study in the coming days,
empowering, regenerating, and indwelling believers. This isn't a rigid
division with no overlap, but a genuine, biblically consistent pattern
in how each person characteristically works.

Notice, too, the specifically Christ-centred content of the Spirit's
role here: he teaches "all things" and reminds believers specifically of
"all that I have said to you" — pointing back to Christ's own words,
not offering some separate or competing revelation. This is worth
underlining, since claims to new spiritual insight are sometimes made in
the Spirit's name that actually contradict or add to Christ's own
teaching. The genuine work of the Spirit always confirms and clarifies
what Christ has already revealed; it never sets up an independent,
rival authority.

Having established who the Spirit is — a person, fully God, in eternal
relationship with the Father and Son, and focused on glorifying Christ —
we turn next to what he does, beginning with his work across creation
and history.

## Prayer
