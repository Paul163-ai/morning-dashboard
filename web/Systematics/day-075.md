## Verse

"The secret things belong to the LORD our God, but the things that are
revealed belong to us and to our children forever, that we may do all
the words of this law." (Deuteronomy 29:29)

## Explanation

Having distinguished God's decreed will from his desired will over the
last two days, the natural question is: how do we actually live with
both in view? Deuteronomy 29:29 gives the practical answer, and it's
refreshingly simple. The secret things — God's hidden, sovereign
purposes, the "why" behind every specific event — belong to him. We are
not given access to them, and we're not meant to spend our lives trying
to reverse-engineer them. What belongs to us is the revealed things: his
commands, his promises, his character as made known in Scripture. Our job
is to obey and trust what's been revealed, not to solve what hasn't.

This single verse quietly dismantles two common but unhealthy patterns.
One is the anxious search for God's "secret plan" for every decision,
treating obedience to his revealed will as insufficient until some
hidden confirmation arrives. The other is fatalistic passivity — using
God's sovereign decree as an excuse not to obey what he's plainly
commanded. Both mistake which category we've actually been given
authority over.

Practically: obey what God has revealed, plainly and without waiting for
more. Trust what he has not revealed, without demanding an explanation.

## Prayer

Lord, the secret things belong to you, and I release my grip on needing
to understand them. Help me simply obey what you have clearly revealed
today. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Are you currently withholding obedience to something God has clearly
revealed, while waiting for clarity on something he hasn't?
