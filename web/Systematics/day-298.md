## Verse

"As for prophecies, they will pass away; as for tongues, they will cease;
as for knowledge, it will pass away." (1 Corinthians 13:8)

## Explanation

In this section we turn to a question that has divided sincere Christians for
generations: are all the spiritual gifts described in the New Testament
still active today, or did some of them, particularly the more dramatic
ones like prophecy, tongues, and healing, cease with the apostolic age?
Those who answer that some gifts have ceased are usually called
cessationists; those who hold that all the gifts continue are called
continuationists. Both positions are held by careful, Bible-believing
Christians, and the debate deserves more care than either side usually
gives it in casual conversation.

Paul's own words here are part of what makes the question genuinely
difficult rather than obvious: he says prophecy and tongues *will* cease,
but ties that cessation to "the perfect" arriving (verse 10), which most
interpreters, on both sides of this debate, take to mean Christ's return
or the completed state of glory, not the closing of the New Testament
canon. That single observation doesn't settle the debate, but it should
make us cautious about resolving it with a single proof text either way.
In the days ahead we'll look honestly at the strongest arguments on both sides.
