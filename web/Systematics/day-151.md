## Verse

"For I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ and him
crucified." — 1 Corinthians 2:2

## Explanation

We close May exactly where Paul chose to centre his own ministry. Writing
to a congregation in Corinth surrounded by rival philosophies and
impressive rhetoric, Paul deliberately narrowed his message to a single
point: Christ, and specifically Christ crucified. Not because other
truths didn't matter, but because this one holds everything else
together — and because, as he says elsewhere in the same letter, it was
precisely the message the world found foolish (1 Corinthians 1:23) that
turned out to be "the power of God and the wisdom of God" (1 Corinthians
1:24).

This month has traced the cross from several angles: the incarnation
that made it possible, Christ's full deity and full humanity united in
one person, his sinless obedience all the way to death, and the rich,
multifaceted meaning of his atoning death — substitution, ransom,
victory, penal satisfaction, sufficient for all and effective for his
own. None of it is a side detail in Christian belief. It's the centre
Paul refused to move past, the fixed point everything else in
Scripture — from Genesis's first promise of a rescuer to Revelation's
final vision of the slain Lamb enthroned — points toward.

As we move into June, tracing Christ's burial, resurrection, ascension,
and offices, and then the Holy Spirit's own person and work, we do so
never leaving this centre behind.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, let Christ crucified remain the centre of everything I
believe, not a truth I move past toward other things. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Looking back over this month, has Christ crucified become more central
to how you think about your faith day to day?
