## Verse

"And this gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed throughout the whole
world as a testimony to all nations, and then the end will come." (Matthew 24:14)

## Explanation

Among the few things Jesus does tie directly to the timing of the end is
this one: the gospel reaching all nations. This connects everything we
studied in the mission of the church, back at the start of this month,
directly to the doctrine of the future — the church's ordinary, patient
work of proclaiming Christ among every people is not incidental to the
end times, it is one of the very things Scripture names as preceding
Christ's return. Mission and eschatology, so often studied separately,
are bound together in Christ's own words.

This gives the church's mission a weight beyond simply obeying a
command; participating in the gospel's advance to every nation is,
however small a part any individual plays in it, participation in
bringing history toward the very end Christians long for. It also
explains why this verse has never functioned as a license for
date-calculation, since "all nations" reached is difficult to verify with
precision and Jesus elsewhere refuses to attach a timeline. What it does
give is purpose: every believer's small part in the Great Commission is
tied to the coming of the very day this whole section has been tracing.

## Prayer

Lord, let the mission I'm part of feel connected to the return I'm
waiting for. Use my small part in reaching the nations to hasten the day
you're bringing history toward. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Does thinking of global mission as tied to Christ's actual return change
how urgently or hopefully you think about it?
