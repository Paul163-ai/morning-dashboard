## Verse

"He who did not spare his own Son but gave him up for us all, how will he
not also with him graciously give us all things?" — Romans 8:32

## Explanation

We're nearly through this whole two-month sequence tracing how salvation
becomes yours — election, calling, regeneration, conversion,
justification, adoption, sanctification, perseverance, glorification —
and union with Christ is what has held every one of them together.
Paul's logic here draws the natural conclusion: if God has already given
you the greatest possible gift — his own Son — then every lesser gift,
everything else you could possibly need, is already secured alongside
that first, greater gift, guaranteed by the same generous purpose.

This is the practical payoff of understanding union with Christ clearly.
You don't need to wonder, gift by gift, blessing by blessing, whether God
will actually follow through — as though each one required a separate
decision, uncertain until it arrives. Union with Christ means you already
possess the whole package, secured together, the moment you were joined
to him. Every specific promise this whole year uncovers is simply drawing
out something already, fully true because of this one central,
foundational union.

Tomorrow, on the final day of August, we'll close this theme and begin
looking ahead to how union with Christ connects to the doctrine of the
church, which we turn to properly in September.

## Prayer

Father, thank you that having given me your own Son, you have already
secured every other gift alongside him. Let that settle any lingering
uncertainty about whether you will follow through on your promises. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Is there a specific promise or blessing you've been anxiously wondering
if God will actually provide? How does today's logic — the greater gift
already given — speak to that?
