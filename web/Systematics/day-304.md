## Verse

"As the Father has sent me, even so I am sending you." — John 20:21

## Explanation

We close October where every doctrine of the church eventually has to
lead: outward. We've spent this month on what happens inside the
gathering — baptism, the Supper, worship, the gifts that build up the
body — but none of it was ever meant to turn the church inward on
itself. Christ's own model for the church's mission is his own sending:
the Father sent the Son into the world, and the Son now sends his
church the same way, into the same world, on the same kind of errand.

That framing matters because it means mission isn't a program the church
runs alongside its real life — evangelism, discipleship, the slow work of
reaching people who don't yet know Christ, is what the gathered church
exists to be equipped for, not a department separate from its worship and
ordinances. The gifts we spent a good stretch of days on, the unity forged at one
table, the word rightly preached — all of it trains and sends a people
outward. We'll spend the coming days tracing exactly what that sending
means, in November, as autumn's doctrine of the church gives way to its
final chapters.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, as the Father sent you, you send your church. Make me willing
to be sent — into my own home, neighbourhood, and workplace — carrying the
gospel that has already reached me. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Where has Christ specifically placed you to be "sent" — not overseas, but
in the ordinary reach of your own life? Are you living as someone sent
there, or just someone who happens to be there?
