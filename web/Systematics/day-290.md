## Verse

"But the hour is coming, and is now here, when the true worshipers will
worship the Father in spirit and truth, for the Father is seeking such
people to worship him." — John 4:23

## Explanation

Jesus said this to a Samaritan woman caught up in an old argument about
the right mountain, the right building, the right location for worship.
His answer relocated the question entirely: true worship is no longer
about geography but about spirit and truth — a heart genuinely engaged
(spirit) responding to God as he has actually revealed himself (truth).
Worship without truth degenerates into sincerity about nothing in
particular; truth without spirit degenerates into correct information that
never touches the heart. Real worship holds both together.

This matters directly for how we think about gathered worship. A service
can be theologically accurate and still fail to be worship, if hearts
never engage; it can be emotionally stirring and still fail to be worship,
if what stirs the emotions isn't actually true about God. The astonishing
claim in this verse is that the Father is *seeking* such worshipers — not
merely tolerating our attempts, but actively desiring genuine engagement
with the truth about who he is. That should shape how seriously we take
both the content and the sincerity of what happens when the church
gathers.

## Prayer

Father, you are seeking worshipers in spirit and truth. Search my heart
the next time I gather with your people — let neither empty emotion nor
empty accuracy stand in for genuine worship of you. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Which comes more naturally to you in worship — engaging your emotions or
attending to truth — and which do you need to grow in?
