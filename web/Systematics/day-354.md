## Verse

"And God saw everything that he had made, and behold, it was very good."
— Genesis 1:31

## Explanation

This section began at Revelation's promise of renewal and has traced its
way through creation's groaning, God's descending presence, and the
curse's total reversal. It's fitting to close by returning to the very
first chapter of Scripture, because the new heavens and new earth are not
a strange, unprecedented invention — they are creation restored to, and
beyond, the goodness this verse first declared. The story of Scripture,
read as a whole, is not a straight line from good to bad to something
entirely different; it is good, corrupted, and then good again, more
fully and permanently than the first time.

This gives shape to everything Christians hope for in the physical
world: not a spiritual abstraction replacing a discarded material
creation, but the very created order God once called very good, restored
to that verdict and secured against ever losing it again. What was true
in Eden before sin entered will be true of the whole renewed earth
forever — and this time, with no serpent left to threaten it. The story
that began with those words in Genesis finally closes having returned
to them, now unbreakable.

## Prayer

Lord, thank you that your final word over creation matches your first —
very good — and this time it will never be undone. Let that hope steady
me as I wait for it. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Does knowing that God's plan has always been restoration, not
replacement, change how you value the physical world and physical life
now?
