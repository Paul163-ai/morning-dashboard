## Verse

"Consequently, he is able to save to the uttermost those who draw near
to God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for
them." — Hebrews 7:25

## Explanation

We close this brief look at the ascension and session with its ongoing,
present-tense significance. Christ's seated reign is not inactive. Right
now, this very moment, the risen and ascended Christ is interceding —
actively representing his people before the Father, exactly as a great
high priest continually does on behalf of those he represents. This
isn't a past, completed action like his death, but a continuous, present
reality: "he always lives to make intercession."

This should be enormously reassuring for anyone who struggles with
ongoing guilt, weakness, or a sense of falling short after conversion.
You are not left to represent yourself before God, relying on your own
current spiritual performance to maintain your standing. You have a
perfect advocate, permanently stationed at the Father's right hand,
continually interceding on your behalf — which is precisely why Hebrews
says he saves "to the uttermost," completely, not merely at the moment
of conversion but all the way through to the end.

This is the practical, ongoing benefit of everything we've traced this
section — his resurrection, ascension, and session aren't only historical
facts to affirm, but the present, active ground of your continued
standing before God, right now, as you read this.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you that you are interceding for me right now, at this
very moment, as my great high priest. Let that ongoing intercession be my
confidence today. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Do you tend to rely on your own current spiritual performance for
confidence before God, rather than resting in Christ's ongoing
intercession?
