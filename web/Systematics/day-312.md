## Verse

"Only let your manner of life be worthy of the gospel of Christ... standing
firm in one spirit, with one mind striving side by side for the faith of
the gospel." (Philippians 1:27)

## Explanation

Paul's language here is the vocabulary of a team, not a collection of
individuals working in parallel: standing firm together, striving side
by side. The Philippian church had partnered with Paul's mission from its
earliest days, sending support, sending a messenger, sharing in his
imprisonment through prayer and gifts (Philippians 4:14–16). Their
mission wasn't something Paul did that they merely funded from a
distance; Paul calls it a genuine partnership, a shared striving for the
same gospel.

This is the shape mission takes at its healthiest: not a lone missionary
and a passive sending church, but a whole body genuinely contending
together, even when only some of its members physically go. Prayer meetings
that specifically and knowledgeably intercede for sent workers, financial
partnership that isn't an afterthought line in a budget, communication
that treats missionaries as fellow strivers rather than distant
recipients of charity: these are what it looks like for a church to
strive side by side rather than merely subsidize from the sidelines.

## Prayer

Lord, make me a genuine partner in your mission, not a passive sponsor of
someone else's. Give me a striving heart for the gospel's advance,
wherever I'm placed. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Do you know the specific needs and struggles of any missionary or mission
work your church supports well enough to pray for them by name and
detail?
