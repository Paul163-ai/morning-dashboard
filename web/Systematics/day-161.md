## Verse

"The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among
you, from your brothers—it is to him you shall listen." (Deuteronomy
18:15)

## Explanation

We turn now to a traditional framework for understanding Christ's
ongoing work: his three offices as Prophet, Priest, and King, roles
that, in the Old Testament, were held by three separate, specially
anointed people, but which converge fully and completely in Christ
alone. We begin with Prophet. Moses himself anticipated a coming
prophet greater than any who followed him, one whose words the people
would be obligated to heed absolutely.

The New Testament identifies Christ directly as this promised prophet
(Acts 3:22-23). A prophet's core role is to speak God's own words to
God's people, and Christ did this not merely by relaying messages from
God the way earlier prophets had, but by being, himself, the very Word
of God we studied back in May (John 1:1, 14). Every previous prophet
said "thus says the LORD"; Christ could simply say "I say to you"
(Matthew 5:22, 28, 32...) with an authority no prior prophet possessed
in himself.

This should shape how seriously we take his words in Scripture. He is
not one voice among many religious teachers offering opinions to weigh
against others. He is the final, definitive prophet, whose words we are
directly commanded to heed above every other voice.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, you are the prophet greater than Moses, speaking with your
own authority. Help me listen to your words above every competing voice
today. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Whose voice competes most for authority in your life alongside Christ's
words, and how seriously are you actually heeding him above it?
