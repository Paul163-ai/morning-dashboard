## Verse

"This God—his way is perfect; the word of the LORD proves true; he is a
shield for all those who take refuge in him." (Psalm 18:30)

## Explanation

We turn now to a short cluster of attributes that describe God's
sheer completeness — perfection, blessedness, beauty, and glory — each
answering, in a different way, the question of whether God lacks
anything at all. Perfection means God fully possesses every desirable
quality, entirely, without any defect or lack. He isn't "very good" the
way an excellent human might be, with room to grow or blind spots to
correct. There's nothing about God still in progress.

Psalm 18 pairs this perfection with something very practical: because
his way is perfect, his word proves true, and he becomes a shield for
those who trust him. Perfection isn't offered here as an abstract
compliment, but as the reason God's promises can be relied on absolutely.
An imperfect God might mean well and still fall short. A perfect God
cannot fall short of what he has said, because there's no gap between
his intention and his ability to follow through.

This attribute is quietly reassuring in a way that's easy to overlook.
Every human relationship, however good, eventually reveals some flaw or
limitation. God is the one relationship where that never happens — not
because he's been tested and passed, but because lacking nothing is
simply what it means to be God.

## Prayer

Perfect God, your way lacks nothing and your word never falls short.
Let me rest today in a relationship that will never disappoint me the
way even good things in this world eventually do. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Where have you unconsciously expected a person or achievement to give
you the completeness only God can provide?
