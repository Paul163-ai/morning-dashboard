## Verse

"Whom God put forward as a propitiation by his blood, to be received by
faith." — Romans 3:25

## Explanation

Propitiation is a dense word carrying an enormous idea: the turning aside
of wrath by the offering of a sacrifice. Yesterday we saw that God's
wrath against sin is real, holy, and presently revealed. Romans 3:25
tells us where that wrath was fully and finally absorbed — not
deflected, not simply forgiven and forgotten, but actually satisfied, in
the death of Christ.

This is different from saying God just decided to stop being angry. If
he had, his own justice — which we studied earlier this month — would
have been compromised; sin would have gone unanswered. Instead, the
wrath sin deserved was poured out fully on Christ in our place, so that
justice was completely satisfied *and* mercy was completely extended,
without either one being sacrificed for the other. This is why the cross
can be called both the darkest and the most loving moment in history at
once.

Received "by faith" means this isn't automatic or universal in
application — it's appropriated, not merely observed. But for everyone
who trusts in Christ, the wrath that was rightly ours has already been
exhausted. There is nothing left for judgment to fall on, because it
already fell — on him.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for absorbing the wrath I deserved so that mercy
could reach me without cost to your justice. Let me never take that
exchange for granted. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Does the reality that Christ absorbed God's wrath in your place change
how you think about your own standing before God today?
