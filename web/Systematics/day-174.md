## Verse

"The earth was without form and void, and darkness was over the face of
the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the
waters." (Genesis 1:2)

## Explanation

We turn now to the Spirit's work across creation and history, beginning
at the very first page of Scripture. Before God speaks light into being,
before any of the ordered creation we studied back in the doctrine of
God takes shape, the Spirit is already present and active: "hovering"
over the formless deep, actively involved in bringing order and life out
of chaos. Creation was never the Father's work alone; Scripture presents
it, from the very first verses, as the shared work of Father, Son (John
1:3, "all things were made through him"), and Spirit together.

This matters because it's easy to think of the Spirit's work as
beginning only at Pentecost, or only in the lives of believers after
conversion. Genesis 1:2 corrects that. The Spirit's active involvement
in the world stretches back to its very beginning, present and working
in the ordering of creation itself, long before any human being existed
to receive him personally.

This should widen our sense of the Spirit's scope. He is not merely a
resource for personal spiritual experience, however real and important
that is. He is the same Spirit who was actively bringing order out of
chaos at the dawn of creation, present, active, and purposeful in the
world's history from its very first moment.

## Prayer

Spirit of God, you were active at creation's very beginning, bringing
order from chaos. Help me see your ongoing work in the world as wide as
that first, foundational act. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Does thinking of the Spirit's work as beginning at creation, not just at
Pentecost, change how you view his activity in the world today?
