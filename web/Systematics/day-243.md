## Verse

"So if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed
away; behold, the new has come." — 2 Corinthians 5:17

## Explanation

We close August here — the same verse that closed our readings on
regeneration back in July, but returned to now with everything the last
two months have added around it. "In Christ" is where every single truth
we've traced actually lives: chosen in him, crucified with him, raised
with him, abiding in him, every blessing secured in him. Your entire
identity as a Christian isn't a collection of separate achievements or
separate gifts, loosely gathered together. It's one identity, "in Christ,"
from which everything else genuinely, naturally flows.

This should be the settled foundation you carry forward as we move, next
month, into an entirely new section: the doctrine of the church. Because
here is the connection worth making before we turn that corner —
everyone who is united to Christ is, by that very union, also united to
every other person who is united to him. Union with Christ isn't a
private, individual reality alone. It's the root of the church itself,
the body he heads, made up of everyone who shares this same "in Christ"
identity — which is exactly where next month begins.

For now, let this whole two-month sequence rest where it belongs: not in
your performance, your consistency, or your feelings on any given day,
but in the settled, secure, unshakeable reality of being in him.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for two months tracing everything that is mine
because I am in you. Let this be the settled ground I stand on going
forward — not my performance, but your finished work and my union with
you. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Looking back over July and August together — election through union
with Christ — what single truth has most reshaped how you see your own
salvation?
