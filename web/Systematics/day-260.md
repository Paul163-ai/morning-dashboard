## Verse

"And he put all things under his feet and gave him as head over all
things to the church, which is his body." (Ephesians 1:22–23)

## Explanation

Before looking at specific human structures of church government, it's
essential to establish who actually holds ultimate authority: Christ
himself, not any human leader, council, or institution. Whatever form of
government a church adopts (and we'll survey the main options in the days
ahead), every legitimate human authority within the church is derivative
and accountable, exercised under Christ's own headship, never replacing
or standing above it.

This has real, practical implications. No pastor, elder board, council,
or denominational structure can claim final, unquestionable authority the
way Christ alone possesses it. Human leaders in the church are servants,
exercising delegated, accountable authority for the good of the flock
(1 Peter 5:2–3), always themselves subject to Christ and his word, never
functioning as a substitute for either. This is why Scripture remains
the final court of appeal for testing any human teaching or leadership
within the church (a truth we established carefully back in January);
church government exists to serve and protect the church's submission
to Christ, never to override it.

This should shape how you relate to leadership in your own church:
genuine respect and submission to legitimate, biblical authority, held
together with the recognition that every human leader remains
accountable to a higher, unshakeable authority than their own position.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, you alone are the head of your church. Help me respect and
submit to the leaders you've given me, while never forgetting that they,
like me, remain accountable to you above all. Amen.

## Reflection Question

How do you currently hold together genuine respect for your church's
leadership with the recognition that Christ alone holds final authority?
