## Verse

"Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within
you, whom you have from God? You are not your own, for you were bought
with a price. So glorify God in your body." (1 Corinthians 6:19-20)

## Explanation

Yesterday's truth (that you are more than your body) could easily be
mistaken for the opposite error: that the body is unimportant, a mere
container for what really matters. Paul rules that out just as firmly.
The body is not a disposable shell to be tolerated until the soul is
freed from it; it is, remarkably, described as "a temple of the Holy
Spirit," a place of God's own dwelling, purchased at the cost of
Christ's own death, and meant to bring him glory.

This corrects a very old and persistent instinct (sometimes called
Gnosticism in its ancient form) that treats spiritual things as
inherently good and physical things as inherently inferior or even bad.
Scripture never allows that split. Christianity's own central hope isn't
an escape from bodily existence into pure spirit, but bodily
resurrection: a renewed, physical, glorified body, in continuity with
the one you have now. The body matters enough to God that he intends
to redeem it, not discard it.

Practically, this shapes how you treat your physical life: what you do
with your body, how you care for it, how you use it, not as
irrelevant to spirituality, but as itself an arena for glorifying God.

## Prayer

Lord, thank you that my body matters to you, purchased and indwelt by
your Spirit. Help me glorify you in how I use and care for my body
today, not treating it as unimportant. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Is there a way you've been treating your body as unimportant to your
spiritual life, rather than as a temple meant to glorify God?
