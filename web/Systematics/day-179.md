## Verse

"You, however, are not in the flesh but in the Spirit, if in fact the
Spirit of God dwells in you." (Romans 8:9)

## Explanation

Regeneration, which we studied yesterday, is a decisive, one-time new
birth. Indwelling describes what follows it: the Spirit's ongoing,
permanent residence within every believer, not a temporary visitation but
a settled dwelling. Paul's language is direct and personal: the Spirit
"dwells in you," echoing the temple imagery we studied back in April
(1 Corinthians 6:19), where a believer's body itself is described as the
Spirit's dwelling place.

This is worth sitting with for what it actually claims. Under the old
covenant, God's manifest presence dwelt in a physical building (first
the tabernacle, then the temple), accessible only to specific priests,
under strict conditions, at specific times. Now, for every believer, that
same presence dwells personally, permanently, within them, without the
mediating structures of the old covenant. This is a staggering upgrade in
access and intimacy, made possible only because of Christ's completed
work, which we studied at length in May.

This should reshape ordinary daily awareness. You are never navigating
temptation, weariness, or decisions alone, relying solely on your own
internal resources. The very Spirit of God dwells within you, personally
and permanently, as close as your own breath.

## Prayer

Holy Spirit, thank you for dwelling within me personally and
permanently, not visiting temporarily. Help me live today aware of your
constant presence. Amen.

## Reflection Question

How would today look different if you were consciously aware, moment by
moment, that the Spirit dwells within you?
