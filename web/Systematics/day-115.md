## Verse

"For from within, out of the heart of man, come evil thoughts, sexual
immorality, theft, murder, adultery, coveting, wickedness, deceit,
sensuality, envy, slander, pride, foolishness. All these evil things
come from within, and they defile a person." (Mark 7:21-23)

## Explanation

Having spent several days on original sin (the inherited condition we
didn't choose), we turn now to actual sin: the specific thoughts, words,
and deeds each of us actually commits. Jesus is correcting a religious
culture obsessed with external ritual purity (the context is handwashing
before meals) by locating the real source of defilement somewhere
deeper: not what goes into a person, but what comes out of the heart
already inside them.

Notice how ordinary and varied Jesus' list is. It includes dramatic sins
(murder, adultery) alongside quieter, more socially acceptable ones
(envy, pride, foolishness, slander), all traced to the same source, the
heart, and all named without a hierarchy that lets the respectable sins
off easily. This connects directly to what we learned about original
sin: actual sins aren't random, disconnected failures; they're the
natural fruit of the corrupted nature we inherited, expressing itself in
a thousand specific ways across an ordinary life.

This should keep us honest about self-examination. It's easy to feel
fairly clean by avoiding the list's more dramatic entries while
tolerating pride, envy, or slander as minor. Jesus draws no such
distinction: all of it defiles, and all of it flows from the same
uncorrected heart.

## Prayer

Lord, examine my heart, not just my outward behaviour. Show me the
quieter sins on Jesus' list that I've been tolerating as harmless. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Which item on Jesus' list (pride, envy, slander, foolishness, or
another) have you been treating as minor compared to more "serious"
sins?
