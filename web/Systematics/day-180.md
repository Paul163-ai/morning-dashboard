## Verse

"In him you also, when you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your
salvation, and believed in him, were sealed with the promised Holy
Spirit, who is the guarantee of our inheritance until we acquire
possession of it, to the praise of his glory." — Ephesians 1:13-14

## Explanation

Alongside regeneration and indwelling, Paul describes the Spirit's
presence in the believer using two vivid commercial images familiar to
his first readers: a seal, marking a document or package as authentic
and secure, guaranteeing it belongs to its rightful owner and protecting
it from tampering; and a guarantee (the Greek word could also be
translated "down payment" or "deposit"), the first installment of a
larger sum still to come, promising the rest will certainly follow.

Both images point to security, not merely presence. The Spirit's
indwelling isn't only about closeness and comfort now — it's God's own
guarantee that the salvation begun in you will certainly be completed,
all the way to your full inheritance (a theme we'll return to later this
year when we study glorification and the resurrection body). Having the
Spirit now is proof, not merely hope, that what has started will be
finished.

This should settle any anxiety about whether your salvation might
somehow slip away or fail to be completed. God himself has already put
down a deposit, sealing you as his own, guaranteeing the rest of the
inheritance is certainly still coming.

## Prayer

Holy Spirit, you are God's own seal and guarantee on my salvation. Let
that certainty settle any fear that what has begun in me might not be
completed. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Do you carry any lingering anxiety about whether your salvation is
secure? How does the image of the Spirit as seal and guarantee address
that?
