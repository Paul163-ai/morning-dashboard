## Verse

"No one has ever seen God; the only God, who is at the Father's side, he
has made him known." — John 1:18

## Explanation

Yesterday we saw Christ as the promised prophet, speaking with his own
divine authority. Today's verse presses further into what that prophetic
role actually accomplishes: making the invisible Father genuinely known.
No prophet before him had ever seen God directly and reported back
firsthand (even Moses was shown only God's back, Exodus 33:20-23); Christ,
uniquely, has been "at the Father's side" from eternity, and it's from
that unique vantage point that he reveals him.

This is worth pairing with something Christ himself said: "Whoever has
seen me has seen the Father" (John 14:9). His prophetic office isn't
limited to his spoken teaching, as important as that is. His entire life
— his character, his compassion, his holiness, his actions — was itself
a revelation of who the Father is. Want to know what God is actually
like? Scripture's answer is not to speculate philosophically, but to
look at Christ.

This should shape how we pursue knowing God. Every accurate thing we
believe about the Father's character ultimately needs to be tested
against what has been revealed in the Son — not because Christ replaces
Scripture's other revelation of God, but because he is its climax and
clearest lens, the one through whom the invisible God has finally, fully
been made known.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for making the Father known — not just in your
words but in your whole life. Let me look to you to know what God is
truly like. Amen.

## Reflection Question

When you picture God's character, are you picturing him through the
lens of Christ's revealed life, or through some other source?
