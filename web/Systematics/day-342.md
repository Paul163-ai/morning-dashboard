## Verse

"To an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in
heaven for you." — 1 Peter 1:4

## Explanation

Every inheritance a person receives in this life carries a shadow: it can
be spent, spoiled, contested, or lost. Peter piles up three words to make
sure his readers grasp that the inheritance believers are promised carries
none of these vulnerabilities — imperishable, incapable of decay;
undefiled, incapable of being spoiled or corrupted; unfading, incapable
of losing its beauty or worth over time. And unlike every earthly
inheritance, which sits vulnerable until the moment it's received, this
one is actively kept, guarded, by God himself, for the specific people it
belongs to.

This is worth sitting with plainly, before the coming days turn to more
specific pictures of what heaven holds: whatever else remains partly
mysterious about the eternal state, its most basic quality is security.
Nothing about this inheritance depends on believers' own strength to
protect or preserve it; the same God who promised it is the one keeping
it safe until the day it is finally received. The anxieties that
naturally attach to anything valuable in this life — loss, theft, decay —
simply don't apply here.

## Prayer

Father, thank you for an inheritance you yourself are keeping safe for me
— one nothing can spoil, corrupt, or take away. Let that security calm
whatever anxious grip I hold on what fades in this life. Amen.

## Reflection Question

What do you tend to hold onto anxiously in this life that this
imperishable, kept inheritance should free you to hold more loosely?
