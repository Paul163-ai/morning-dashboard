## Verse

"You are the salt of the earth... You are the light of the world. A city
set on a hill cannot be hidden." (Matthew 5:13–14)

## Explanation

Salt and light both do their work by contact, not by isolation. Salt
preserves and flavours only when it is actually mixed into the food; kept
in the shaker, sealed away from anything it might touch, it does nothing.
Light illuminates only when it shines outward into a dark room; hidden
under a basket, it might as well not exist. Jesus's images push the
church toward the world it is tempted to withdraw from, not away from it:
presence, not isolation, is the posture mission requires.

This is a needed corrective in both directions. A church that isolates
itself from the surrounding culture, however well-intentioned the
motives, has hidden its light exactly where Jesus said not to put it. But
a church that blends in so completely it loses its distinctiveness has
lost its saltiness: flavour that has become indistinguishable from what
surrounds it is no longer worth anything, "good for nothing except to be
thrown out" (5:13). The calling is neither withdrawal nor assimilation,
but distinct presence: genuinely in the world, genuinely different from
it.

## Prayer

Lord, keep me present in the world you've placed me in, without losing the
distinctiveness that makes my presence actually worth something. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Are you currently more tempted toward isolation from the people around
you, or toward blending in so much that nothing about you looks different?
