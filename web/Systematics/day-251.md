## Verse

"And he gave the apostles, the prophets, the evangelists, the shepherds
and teachers, to equip the saints for the work of ministry, for building
up the body of Christ." (Ephesians 4:11–12)

## Explanation

Alongside worship, the church exists for edification: building up its
own members, growing them toward maturity in Christ. Paul describes this
happening through specific gifted leaders, whose task isn't to do all
the ministry themselves on behalf of a passive congregation, but to equip
ordinary believers for their own genuine ministry to one another. The
church, rightly functioning, is a place where every member is being
actively built up, and where every member also contributes to building
up others.

This matters because it's easy to attend a church for years as a purely
passive consumer, receiving teaching, benefiting from community, without
ever actively contributing to anyone else's growth. Scripture's picture
of edification is mutual and active: "encourage one another" (1
Thessalonians 5:11), "stir up one another to love and good
works" (Hebrews 10:24), "teach and admonish one another" (Colossians
3:16), dozens of "one another" commands throughout the New Testament,
assuming every believer has something genuine to contribute to the
growth of others.

This should reshape your own expectations of church involvement. Not
simply "what am I receiving here," but "who am I actively building up,
and who is actively building me up in return?"

## Prayer

Lord, thank you for the gift of a church built to grow together. Show me
who I could actively encourage or build up in the days ahead, not just receive
from. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Can you name one specific person you're currently helping to build up in
their faith, and one person actively building you up?
