## Verse

"All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore
and make disciples of all nations... teaching them to observe all that I
have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the
age." (Matthew 28:18–20)

## Explanation

We open November still inside the church's mission, which we began
tracing on the last day of October. The Great Commission is worth reading
in full, because its shape is easy to flatten into a single command when
it is actually three things held together: an authority (Christ's, total
and universal), a command (make disciples, not merely converts, but
students who learn to obey everything Christ taught), and a promise (his
own presence, to the very end).

Notice, too, that the command is not "make decisions" but "make
disciples," and the verb that follows is "teaching them to observe," an
ongoing, patient process, not a single moment of response. The church's
mission was never meant to end at the point someone believes; it continues
through baptism (which we studied in October) and through the slow work
of teaching obedience, for as long as this age lasts. That long horizon is
only bearable because of the promise attached to it: Christ himself,
present with his church, all the way to the end.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, you hold all authority, and you have sent me with a promise of
your presence. Give me courage to make disciples, not just converts, and
patience for the slow work of teaching obedience. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Is there someone in your life you've helped come to faith but never
followed up with to help them grow in obedience? What would a next step
look like?
