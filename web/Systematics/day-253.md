## Verse

"Preach the word; be ready in season and out of season; reprove, rebuke,
and exhort, with complete patience and teaching." (2 Timothy 4:2)

## Explanation

In this section we ask a more discerning question: not just what the church is
for, but what actually distinguishes a genuine, true church from a
gathering that merely resembles one. The Reformers identified specific
"marks" of a true church: visible signs by which a genuine church could
be recognised. The first and most fundamental is the faithful preaching
and teaching of God's word, unashamedly and consistently, as the actual
authority shaping the church's life.

This matters because church history is full of gatherings that retained
Christian language, buildings, and traditions while quietly abandoning
Scripture's actual authority, replacing genuine biblical preaching with
moralism, entertainment, political messaging, or vague spirituality
detached from the text. A church can have excellent music, impressive
buildings, and warm community, and still fail this most basic test if
Scripture itself is no longer what's actually being taught and
submitted to from the front.

This gives you a genuinely useful, discerning question to ask of any
church, including your own: is God's actual word (not merely
Christian-sounding themes loosely inspired by it) what's regularly,
faithfully being taught and applied here? Everything else about church
life ultimately depends on getting this foundational mark right.

## Prayer

Lord, thank you for giving your church your own word to preach and
teach. Let my own church remain faithful to this mark, and let me be a
person who genuinely wants to hear your word, not just be entertained.
Amen.

## Reflection Question

When you think honestly about the preaching you regularly sit under, is
it genuinely Scripture being taught and applied, or something adjacent to
it?
