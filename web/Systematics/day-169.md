## Verse

"For he must reign until he has put all his enemies under his feet." —
1 Corinthians 15:25

## Explanation

We close this brief look at Christ's kingship, and our study of his
three offices together, with Paul's summary of exactly how the "already,
but not yet" tension we noted yesterday will resolve. Christ's reign is
not static or merely defensive; it is actively, progressively
subduing every hostile power, moving steadily toward a point where every
last enemy — including death itself, which Paul names specifically in
the very next verse (1 Corinthians 15:26) — will be finally and visibly
defeated.

This gives the prophetic, priestly, and kingly offices their full,
combined shape. As Prophet, Christ has spoken God's final word to us. As
Priest, he has offered the completed sacrifice that secures our access
to God and continues interceding for us even now. As King, he is
actively reigning, progressively subduing every enemy, moving history
toward its guaranteed conclusion. None of these offices operates in
isolation — together they describe the full scope of who Christ is and
what he continues to do, right now, on behalf of his people.

As we move from Christ's own person and offices into the person and work
of the Holy Spirit for the rest of this month, this is the note to carry
forward: the King who reigns even now has also sent his Spirit to be
with his people in the meantime — the very theme we turn to next.

## Prayer
