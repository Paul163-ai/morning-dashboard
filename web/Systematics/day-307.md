## Verse

"So Paul and Barnabas, having been sent on their way by the church,
passed through Phoenicia and Samaria." — Acts 15:3

## Explanation

It is easy to think of "missionaries" as a specialist category — a small
number of especially called believers who go, while everyone else stays
and supports them financially from a distance. Acts pictures something
closer than that. The church at Antioch didn't merely fund Paul and
Barnabas's journey; the text says the Holy Spirit set them apart from the
gathered congregation, the church laid hands on them, and the church sent
them out (Acts 13:2–3) — mission was an act of the whole body,
recognising and releasing gifts already present within it, not
outsourcing a task to specialists.

That reframes the question every believer should ask about mission, not
"am I called to go," which most will answer no to, but "how am I
participating in my church's sending" — through prayer, through
hospitality toward missionaries home on leave, through giving that makes
their going possible, through actively identifying and encouraging the
Pauls and Barnabases already sitting in the pew next to you. Every
Christian has a role in the mission; not every Christian's role is to
leave home.

## Prayer

Lord, show me my part in my church's sending, whether that means going or
enabling others to go. Let me never treat mission as someone else's job.
Amen.

## Reflection Question

What is one concrete way — prayer, giving, hospitality, encouragement —
you could support your church's sent workers this month?
