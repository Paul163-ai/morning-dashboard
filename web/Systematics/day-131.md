## Verse

"And Jesus increased in wisdom and in stature and in favor with God and
man." — Luke 2:52

## Explanation

We close this short section on Christ's humanity with one of the most
understated verses about him in all of Scripture. Luke doesn't describe
a miraculous, fully-formed adult wisdom appearing in the boy Jesus. He
describes ordinary, real growth — in wisdom, in physical stature, in
relationship with God and with people — the normal, gradual development
of a genuine human childhood into adulthood.

This might seem like a small detail, but it closes an important gap.
Christ's full humanity wasn't merely a body inhabited by an already
fully-formed divine mind, skipping past ordinary human development. He
grew, the way every human child grows — learning, maturing, developing
in relationship, subject to time in a way his eternal, unchanging
divine nature (which we studied in detail back in February) is not. This
is part of the mystery we'll examine more closely in the coming days:
how one person could be, at the very same time, eternally unchanging in
his divine nature and genuinely growing in his human nature.

For now, simply notice how thoroughly ordinary this description is.
The eternal Son of God had a childhood indistinguishable, in its basic
shape, from any other — proof that his humanity, like his deity, was
never merely apparent, but entirely, unreservedly real.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for a genuinely human childhood, growing as I
grow. Let your real, lived humanity give me confidence that you know
this human life from the inside. Amen.

## Reflection Question

What difference does it make to you that Jesus experienced ordinary
human growth and development, rather than arriving fully formed?
