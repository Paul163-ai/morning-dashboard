## Verse

"For now we see in a mirror dimly, but then face to face. Now I know in
part; then I shall know fully, even as I have been fully known." — 1
Corinthians 13:12

## Explanation

We close this whole year of doctrine at the destination every doctrine
has ultimately been pointing toward: not information about God, but
unmediated fellowship with him. Everything studied across these twelve
months — Scripture, God's character, humanity, Christ and the Spirit, the
whole work of salvation, the church, the last things — has been the mirror
Paul describes here: real, true, but indirect, dim compared to what is
coming. The goal was never simply to know facts about God more
accurately, though that mattered along the way; it was always this,
knowing him face to face.

Paul's phrase "known fully, even as I have been fully known" reverses the
usual direction we think about knowledge of God. Long before we knew
him, however dimly, he already knew us completely — Psalm 139's "you have
searched me and known me" is not a future hope but a present, already
established fact. What changes at the resurrection isn't God's knowledge
of us, which has always been complete, but ours of him, finally matching
the intimacy he has held toward us all along.

## Prayer

Lord, thank you that you have always known me fully, even while I knew
you only dimly. I long for the day my knowledge of you finally matches
yours of me. Amen.

## Reflection Question

What is one thing about God you currently know only "dimly" that you
most look forward to knowing face to face?
