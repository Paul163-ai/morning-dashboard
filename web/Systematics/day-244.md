## Verse

"To them God chose to make known how great among the Gentiles are the
riches of the glory of this mystery, which is Christ in you, the hope of
glory." (Colossians 1:27)

## Explanation

We've spent this whole past section tracing what it means to be "in
Christ": chosen, crucified, raised, abiding, blessed, all located within
union with him. Today, the same truth from the other direction: not only
are you in Christ, but Christ is in you. Paul calls this a "mystery,"
not because it's confusing, but because it's a staggering reality,
previously hidden, now revealed: the risen, glorified Son of God actually
dwelling within ordinary believers, by his Spirit.

This reciprocal indwelling (you in him, he in you) is the deepest
possible description of what union with Christ actually means. It isn't
a legal fiction, a distant status, or merely an idea to affirm. It's real,
personal, spiritual presence, in both directions: your whole existence
now located within him, and his own life and presence now dwelling within
you. This is why Paul can describe the Christian life, in the same
letter, both as being "hidden with Christ in God" (3:3) and as "Christ
who is your life" (3:4), union so close that your life and his have
become, in a real spiritual sense, inseparable.

This is the "hope of glory" Paul names, not a hope resting on your own
resources, but on the actual presence of Christ himself, already dwelling
within you, guaranteeing the fuller glory still to come.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, thank you for the mystery of dwelling within me by your
Spirit, not distant, but present, my very life. Let me live today aware
of your actual presence in me, not just my presence in you. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Does "Christ in you" feel like a real, present reality to you day to day,
or more like a theological idea you affirm without feeling?
