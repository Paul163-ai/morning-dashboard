## Verse

"Behold, he is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see him, even
those who pierced him, and all tribes of the earth will wail on account of
him." — Revelation 1:7

## Explanation

Some claim Christ's return will be a hidden, secret event, discernible
only to a select few. John's vision says the opposite: every eye will see
him — a universal, unmistakable, public event, visible to believers and
unbelievers alike, including, remarkably, those who pierced him at the
crucifixion. This is not a quiet event unfolding in private for the
initiated; it is the most public event in human history, impossible for
anyone alive at the time to miss or dismiss.

The response John records is equally striking: all the tribes of the
earth will wail. Christ's return will not be received by everyone with
joy. For those who have refused him, his open, undeniable arrival will be
occasion for grief and dread, precisely because his return settles, once
and for all, every question they had managed to keep unresolved. This is
part of why the church proclaims Christ's coming as good news — not
because everyone will welcome it, but because those united to him have
nothing to wail about when the one they've longed for finally, visibly
arrives.

## Prayer

Lord, let your return be something I long for, not dread. Keep me ready,
united to you, so that the day every eye sees you is a day of joy for me,
not wailing. Amen.

## Reflection Question

If Christ returned visibly today, would your first reaction be joy or
fear? What does that reveal about where your hope is currently anchored?
