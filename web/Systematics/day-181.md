## Verse

"But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness,
goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control; against such things
there is no law." (Galatians 5:22-23)

## Explanation

We close June, and this year's entire study of Christ and the Holy
Spirit together, with the Spirit's ongoing work of transformation. Notice
Paul's word: fruit, singular, not fruits: a single, unified character,
naturally produced, rather than a checklist of separate achievements to
strive for independently. And notice, too, that it is fruit of the
*Spirit*, not fruit of willpower or self-discipline. Just as a tree
doesn't strain to produce apples but simply bears them as the natural
outflow of healthy roots, this character is what naturally grows in a
life genuinely indwelt and cultivated by the Spirit.

This is a fitting note to end on, because it ties together everything
this quarter has traced. Christ's incarnation, sinless obedience, and
atoning death secured salvation; his resurrection, ascension, and
ongoing reign as Prophet, Priest, and King guarantee and apply it; and
the Spirit (regenerating, indwelling, sealing) now produces, in
ordinary daily life, the actual character of Christ himself in those he
indwells. Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,
gentleness, self-control: this is what the Spirit's presence looks like
worked out in a real, ordinary life.

As we move into July and the doctrine of salvation's application, this
is where we stand: united to a risen, reigning Christ, indwelt by his
own Spirit, already being shaped, fruit by fruit, into his likeness.

## Prayer

Holy Spirit, produce your fruit in me, not by my straining effort, but
as the natural outflow of your presence within me. Shape me into
Christ's likeness. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Looking back over this month's study of the Spirit, which fruit (love,
joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness,
self-control) do you most need him to grow in you right now?
