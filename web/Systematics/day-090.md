## Verse

"And Jesus said to him, 'If you can! All things are possible for one who
believes.'" (Mark 9:23)

## Explanation

We end March here, at the edge of a desperate father's request for his
suffering son and Jesus' response to it. The father had asked, almost
apologetically, "if you can do anything, have compassion on us" (Mark
9:22). Jesus turns the question back on him: the issue was never his
ability — that was never in doubt across everything we've studied this
month, from his omnipotence to his sovereign providence — the issue is
whether the father will believe.

This is a fitting close to a month spent tracing God's mercy, holiness,
justice, wrath satisfied at the cross, sovereign will, omnipotence,
perfection, providence, and power to work miracles. All of it converges
on a single, practical posture: expectant faith. Not presumption that
demands God act on our timetable or in the specific way we've decided is
best, but genuine confidence that the God we've spent a month describing
is fully able, fully good, and fully worth trusting with whatever we
bring him.

The father's own response is worth remembering as we go: "I believe;
help my unbelief!" (Mark 9:24). That's not a failed prayer — it's an
honest one. As April begins and we turn from the doctrine of God to the
doctrine of man, we carry this forward: a God this great is also a God
worth bringing every genuine doubt to, without pretending we have more
faith than we do.

## Prayer

Lord, I believe; help my unbelief. Thank you for a month spent
rediscovering how great, how good, and how trustworthy you are. Carry
that confidence with me into what's next. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Looking back over this month's study of God's character, what single
attribute has most strengthened your expectant faith in him?
