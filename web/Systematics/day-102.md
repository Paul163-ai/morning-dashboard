## Verse

"Male and female he created them, and he blessed them and named them
Man." — Genesis 5:2

## Explanation

We turn now to a specific facet of the image of God worth its own
attention: humanity as male and female. Genesis 5:2 restates the
creation account with a detail worth noticing — both sexes together are
blessed by God, and both together are named "Man" (Hebrew *adam*,
humankind). Neither sex is an afterthought, a lesser reflection of the
image, or merely instrumental to the other. Both, equally and fully,
bear God's image; both, together, receive his blessing.

This matters because throughout history — and often still today — one
sex or the other has been treated as more fully human, more capable of
reason, more worthy of dignity, or more central to God's purposes than
the other. Genesis 5:2 quietly refuses all of that. Whatever
distinctions Scripture goes on to describe between men and women (a
theme for tomorrow), those distinctions never touch equal dignity, equal
image-bearing, and equal blessing. Galatians 3:28 makes the same point
in a different key: in Christ, there is no hierarchy of worth between
male and female.

Holding this truth clearly guards against two errors at once: erasing
real differences between men and women in the name of equality, or using
real differences as an excuse to treat either sex as less fully in God's
image. Equal dignity and real distinction are not in tension in
Scripture — both are simply true.

## Prayer

Lord, thank you that you made both men and women fully in your image,
equally blessed, equally dignified. Help me honour that equality in how I
treat everyone around me. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Have you ever, even subtly, treated one sex as more fully reflecting
God's image or worth than the other?
