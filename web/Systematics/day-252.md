## Verse

"As the Father has sent me, even so I am sending you." — John 20:21

## Explanation

The third major purpose of the church, alongside worship and edification,
is mission — being sent, as Christ was sent, into the world with the
gospel. A church turned entirely inward, focused only on its own worship
and its own members' growth, has only half its purpose in view. Jesus'
words to his disciples establish the pattern that has shaped the church
ever since: just as the Father sent the Son into the world, the Son sends
his people, in turn, outward.

This sending isn't reserved for a specialised few — missionaries,
pastors, evangelists — even though some are specifically gifted and
called to particular kinds of gospel work, as we saw yesterday. Every
believer, gathered into the church for worship and edification, is also,
by that same membership, part of a body that exists to be sent — carrying
the gospel to neighbours, coworkers, family, and, through the church's
collective efforts, to the ends of the earth.

Holding all three purposes together — worship, edification, mission —
prevents any single one from becoming distorted in isolation. A church
can't rightly worship a God it isn't also proclaiming to those who don't
know him, can't rightly build up its members while ignoring those
outside its walls, and can't rightly pursue mission without the worship
and edification that sustain it for the task.

## Prayer

Lord, as you sent your Son, you send your church, and you send me. Help
me see my ordinary life — my neighbours, my workplace, my relationships —
as part of your sending, not separate from it. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Who in your everyday life could you see as someone you've been
specifically "sent" to, whether or not you've thought of it that way
before?
