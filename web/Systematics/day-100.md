## Verse

"Then God said, 'Let us make man in our image, after our likeness. And let
them have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the
heavens and over the livestock and over all the earth...'" — Genesis 1:26

## Explanation

Yesterday we established that humanity uniquely bears God's image.
Today's verse fills in some of what that actually includes. Notice the
image is introduced in the very same breath as "dominion" — a
God-given responsibility to rule over and care for creation on God's
behalf. Bearing God's image isn't merely a private, internal quality; it
comes with a task, a representative role, similar to how an ancient king
might place his image throughout his territory to signify his rule was
present even where he wasn't physically standing.

Theologians have long discussed exactly which human capacities most
reflect this image — moral reasoning, rational thought, relational
capacity, spiritual awareness, creativity, dominion over creation are
all commonly named. Rather than pinning the image down to one narrow
faculty, it's probably best understood as something closer to the whole
person: every part of what makes us distinctly human — capable of
knowing God, relating to others in love, creating, reasoning, ruling
responsibly — reflects, however imperfectly this side of the fall, the
God who made us this way.

This should elevate how we think about ordinary human capacities and
work. Creativity, responsible stewardship of resources, thoughtful
reasoning, caring relationships — these aren't merely useful skills.
They're small echoes of the God whose image we bear.

## Prayer

Lord, thank you that bearing your image touches every part of who I am
— my mind, my relationships, my work, my care for what you've entrusted
to me. Help me steward all of it well today. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Which of your ordinary daily activities — work, creativity, relationships,
care for your surroundings — could you start seeing as reflecting God's
image rather than just getting through the day?
