## Verse

"He entered once for all into the holy places, not by means of the blood
of goats and calves but by means of his own blood, thus securing an
eternal redemption." — Hebrews 9:12

## Explanation

A priest's central task is to offer sacrifice — and Christ's priestly
office is unique precisely in what, and whom, he offered. Every Old
Testament priest offered an animal, standing outside the sacrifice
itself, repeating the act because animal blood could never actually
remove sin, only temporarily cover it (Hebrews 10:4). Christ, uniquely,
is both the priest offering the sacrifice and the sacrifice itself,
offering "his own blood," and doing so "once for all" — a single act,
never needing repetition, because it actually, fully accomplished what
it was offered to accomplish.

This is the priestly office giving fuller shape to the atonement we
studied in depth last month. Substitution, penal satisfaction, the
finished work declared from the cross — all of it is the content of what
Christ's unique, priestly self-offering achieved. No other priest could
have offered themselves this way, because no other priest was both
sinless enough to qualify as an acceptable sacrifice and divine enough
for that sacrifice to carry infinite worth.

This should deepen, not merely repeat, our gratitude from last month. It
wasn't only that Christ died for us — it's that he acted as both the
priest performing the offering and the offering itself, a role no one
else in all of history could have filled.

## Prayer

Lord Jesus, you were both priest and sacrifice, offering yourself once
for all. No one else could have done what only you could accomplish.
Amen.

## Reflection Question

Does understanding Christ as both priest and sacrifice add depth to how
you think about the cross beyond what you already knew?
