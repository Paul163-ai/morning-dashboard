## Verse

"Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the
schemes of the devil." (Ephesians 6:11)

## Explanation

We close this brief study of spiritual warfare (and this month's whole
tour through the doctrine of God) with Paul's practical answer to
yesterday's diagnosis. If the real battle is spiritual, ordinary
strength isn't sufficient equipment; what's needed is armour God himself
supplies. Notice Paul's aim isn't victory through aggression, but
standing firm: truth, righteousness, readiness from the gospel of peace,
faith, salvation, and the Spirit's own word (Ephesians 6:14-17); every
piece defensive or given, none of it self-generated.

This is a fitting note to end on. Everything we've studied about God this
month (his mercy and holiness, his wrath satisfied at the cross, his
sovereign will, his omnipotence, his providence even over evil, his power
to work miracles) is precisely the equipment Paul describes. The
"armour of God" isn't a separate resource alongside the character of God;
it's that character, put on and relied upon in the actual moments of
opposition.

As we move from the doctrine of God into the doctrine of man tomorrow,
this is worth carrying forward: knowing who God is was never meant to
stay abstract. It's meant to be worn: trusted, depended on, and stood
in, especially when opposition comes.

## Prayer

Lord, clothe me today in your own truth, righteousness, and peace. Let
everything I've learned of your character this month become armour I
actually stand in, not just truths I've studied. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Which piece of God's armour (truth, righteousness, peace, faith,
salvation, the Spirit's word) do you most need to put on today?
