## Verse

"He is the radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his
nature, and he upholds the universe by the word of his power." (Hebrews 1:3)

## Explanation

We turn now to providence — God's continuing, active involvement in
sustaining and governing everything he has made. It's worth
distinguishing providence from creation, though the two are related.
Creation was God bringing the universe into being out of nothing.
Providence is what he's been doing every moment since: not stepping back
to let a self-sufficient universe run on its own momentum, but actively
holding it together, right now, "by the word of his power."

This is a bigger claim than it first sounds. It means the laws of
physics aren't independent forces that happen to constrain God's
creation from the outside; they're the regular, dependable pattern of
God's own ongoing sustaining activity. Every atom that holds together,
every law that reliably governs matter, does so because God continues
to uphold it moment by moment — not because the universe, once launched,
can now run itself.

This attribute (sometimes called preservation) grounds something we take
for granted every day: that the world will still be here, working the
same way, when we wake up tomorrow. That reliability isn't a brute fact
about nature. It's a daily gift from a God who has never stopped holding
his creation together, and has given us no reason to expect he ever
will.

## Prayer

Sustaining God, thank you that you uphold the universe, and my life
within it, by your own power every moment. Help me trust your ongoing
care rather than taking it for granted. Amen.

## Reflection Question

What part of your daily life do you take for granted as "just how things
work," rather than recognising as God's ongoing sustaining care?
