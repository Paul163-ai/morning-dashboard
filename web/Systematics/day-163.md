## Verse

"He was still speaking when, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them,
and a voice from the cloud said, 'This is my beloved Son, with whom I am
well pleased; listen to him.'" — Matthew 17:5

## Explanation

We close this short look at Christ's prophetic office at the
transfiguration — a moment when Moses and Elijah, representing the Law
and the Prophets, appear alongside Jesus, and then, remarkably, fade
from the scene, leaving Christ alone as the Father's voice declares:
"listen to him." This is a deliberate, dramatic statement about how
Christ's prophetic office relates to everything that came before it.
The Old Testament law and prophets were genuine, authoritative
revelation from God — and yet they pointed forward to, and are now
fulfilled and surpassed by, the one now standing alone before the
Father's endorsement.

This doesn't discard the earlier revelation Moses and Elijah represent
— Christ himself said he came to fulfill, not abolish, the Law and the
Prophets (Matthew 5:17). But it does establish a clear priority: when
any voice, however ancient or authoritative, seems to compete with
Christ's own word, the Father's direct command is simple — listen to
him. He is the final word, the one every previous revelation was always
pointing toward.

This closes our study of the prophetic office where it began: Christ's
words carry an authority no other voice, however respected, can rival.

## Prayer

Father, thank you for your command to listen to your Son above every
other voice. Let his words settle every question competing revelations
might raise. Amen.

Having spent three days on Christ as Prophet, how has your view of the
