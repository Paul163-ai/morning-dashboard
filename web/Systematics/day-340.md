## Verse

"The one who rejects me and does not receive my words has a judge; the
word that I have spoken will judge him on the last day." — John 12:48

## Explanation

Jesus locates the standard of final judgment not in some hidden, unknown
criterion but in something already given and available: his own words,
already spoken, already offered to be received or rejected. This means
the last day's verdict will not spring any surprises about the standard
being used — everyone judged will be judged by exactly the revelation
they already had access to, whether that was the fuller light of the
gospel clearly preached to them, or the dimmer light of creation and
conscience for those who never heard it (Romans 1:20, 2:14–15).

This should reshape how urgently we take proclaiming Christ's words now,
while there is still time for them to be received rather than merely
judged by. Every gospel presentation, every faithful teaching of
Scripture, every patient answer to a friend's honest question, is placing
the very standard of the last day's judgment into someone's hands while
it can still function as an invitation rather than only a verdict. The
words that will judge on the last day are, right now, still doing their
first and better work: offering life to anyone who will receive them.

## Prayer

Lord, thank you that your word is not hidden from me. Let me receive it
now as an invitation, and help me offer it to others while it can still
be received rather than only faced. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Is there someone who has heard the words of Christ from you before but
hasn't yet responded? What would it look like to offer them again, while
there's still time?
