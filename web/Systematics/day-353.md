## Verse

"No longer will there be anything accursed, but the throne of God and of
the Lamb will be in it." — Revelation 22:3

## Explanation

This single sentence undoes what Genesis 3 put in place. When Adam and
Eve sinned, God pronounced a curse reaching the ground itself — thorns,
thistles, painful labour, a creation subjected to futility, exactly what
Paul described a few days ago as creation's groaning. Every hardship this
world has known since — disease, disaster, decay, death — traces back to
that one pronouncement. John's vision announces its complete reversal:
not merely reduced, not merely managed, but no longer present at all.

This is the specific hope that makes the new heavens and new earth more
than a vague promise of improvement. It is the undoing of the very thing
that broke the world in the first place, all the way back to its root.
Every grief this year's readings have touched — death, tribulation,
suffering, a groaning creation — traces its origin to that ancient curse,
and every one of those griefs is answered here, at the very end of
Scripture's story, by its total removal. The throne of God and the Lamb,
present at last where the curse once reigned, is the reason nothing
accursed can remain.

## Prayer

Lord, thank you for undoing, completely, what sin broke at the beginning.
Let this promise of "no longer" comfort every grief I carry from a world
still under that curse now. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Which specific effect of the curse — disease, decay, death, difficult
labour — do you most long to see reversed, for yourself or someone you
love?
