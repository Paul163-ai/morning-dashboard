## Verse

"He chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be
holy and blameless before him." — Ephesians 1:4

## Explanation

We return to a verse we studied back in July, on election, but see it
now through the specific lens of union with Christ: God's choice of you
was never a choice of you in isolation, considered separately and then
somehow connected to Christ afterward. You were chosen "in him" — your
election itself already located within union with Christ, from before
the world began. There was never a version of your salvation that didn't
already involve being joined to him.

This reshapes how election, back in July, should actually be understood.
It wasn't God selecting individuals based on some quality he found
appealing in them apart from Christ. It was God's eternal purpose to have
a people united to his Son, and choosing you meant, necessarily and
simultaneously, choosing you to be found in Christ — the whole
relationship, not a separate decision made about you alone.

This is why your standing before God has never rested on anything
found in you, examined in isolation. It rests entirely on being found
"in him" — a location, a relationship, established before creation
itself, that every other blessing this whole year has traced flows from.

## Prayer

Father, thank you that you chose me already in Christ, not separately
from him. Let my confidence rest entirely on being found in him, not on
anything you might find examining me alone. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Does thinking of your election as "in Christ" rather than as a separate
decision about you individually change how secure it feels?
