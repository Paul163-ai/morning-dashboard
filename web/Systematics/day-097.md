## Verse

"For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers,
against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present
darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places." —
Ephesians 6:12

## Explanation

Paul writes this after a long letter about unity, holiness, and
relationships within the church — and precisely at that point he pulls
back the curtain on the deeper conflict underneath. The struggles
believers face — conflict, temptation, discouragement, division — often
have visible, human faces attached to them. Paul insists the ultimate
opposition is not against "flesh and blood" at all, but against
organised spiritual forces of evil working behind the human scene.

This is not an excuse to avoid responsibility for our own sin, or to
treat every difficult person in our lives as merely a puppet of demonic
forces beyond their control — Scripture holds humans fully accountable
for their own choices elsewhere. What Paul is doing is reframing where
our fundamental fight actually lies. Bitterness toward a difficult
colleague, resentment in a marriage, division within a church — these
have real human elements, but Paul wants us to see the spiritual battle
underneath, so our response isn't purely tactical (fixing the visible
symptom) but spiritual (recognising and resisting the deeper opposition).

This sets up tomorrow's practical instruction directly. If the battle is
spiritual, ordinary human strength and cleverness were never going to be
sufficient weapons for it.

## Prayer

Lord, help me see past the human faces of my struggles to the spiritual
battle underneath, without excusing sin or blaming others unfairly. Amen.

## Reflection Question

Is there a conflict or struggle in your life you've been fighting purely
on a human level, that needs to be recognised as spiritual too?
